# brawl-rl

Neural bots you can play against, in the browser (phones too).

**Play: https://yozh2709.github.io/brawl-rl/**

Mortis vs Mortis, 1v1 Brawl Ball, against a small net trained with self-play PPO in a simulator.
The game and the net run in your browser; no server.
Two nets, **Net A** and **Net B**: pick one in the menu at the top. Which one is better is the question.

## Controls

- **PC:** WASD move, mouse aim, left click attack (dash), right click super, E gadget, Q hypercharge, Enter new game.
- **Phone:** move stick anywhere on the left. ATK / SUPER: drag to aim, release to fire; a tap aims for you
  (at their goal when you hold the ball, else at the nearest enemy you see). GADGET and HYPER are taps.

After a game, **⬇ replay** saves it as a .json file. Send it along with your feedback.
